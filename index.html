Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for the grant?
Individuals and families in USA, Canada, and Australia who meet the program requirements. Each grant program has specific eligibility criteria.
How long does the application process take?
The review process typically takes 5-10 business days. You'll receive a notification once a decision is made.
What documents do I need to apply?
You'll need a valid government-issued ID, proof of address, and any additional documents specific to your chosen grant program.
Can I apply for multiple grants?
Yes, you can apply for multiple grant programs as long as you meet the eligibility requirements for each.
